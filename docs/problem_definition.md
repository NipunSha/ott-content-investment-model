# Problem definition
**Decision:** a hypothetical mid-sized Indian OTT platform has budget for one six-episode scripted series and can (A) commission it, (B) license an existing title, or (C) co-produce with a partner.

**Question:** under what financial and commercial conditions is commissioning preferable to acquiring or co-producing?

**Why it matters:** Indian platforms have cut per-episode budgets and commissioning volumes since 2022 (trade press, S05/S06) while paid subscriptions keep growing (FICCI-EY, S01). The allocation question is therefore live, and it turns on rights and risk as much as on headline cost.

**Unit of analysis:** one title, evaluated on incremental cash contribution over 36 months after launch, discounted to the decision date.

**Out of scope:** estimating how many subscribers a real title would attract; catalogue-level portfolio effects; tax and accounting amortisation.
